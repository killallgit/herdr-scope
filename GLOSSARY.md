# Herdr Scope

Scope presents agents in the Agents panel and workspaces in Spaces.

## Language

**Workspace**:
A Herdr workspace, shown as a Space, containing tabs and panes.

**Family**:
A workspace and the workspaces nested beneath it by repository or folder. A family remains one group even when its parent runs no agent.

**Fold**:
A remembered choice to condense a family's agents while keeping questions, unseen results, focused agents, agents in the focused workspace, and a lone agent visible.

**Fold line**:
The condensed summary of a family: its name, hidden-agent count, and activity.

**Carrier**:
The agent whose place in the Agents panel carries a fold line. When the line replaces that agent's own row, focusing the carrier opens the family for a visit.

**Visit**:
A temporary opening of a folded family that lasts until focus leaves the family; the remembered fold remains.
